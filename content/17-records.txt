Reconciliation shows whether recorded activity agrees with actual cash movement; it does not by itself establish correct tax treatment. Should I separate rental and personal records?|Clear separation makes business activity easier to understand and helps identify personal use or mixed expenses. How do I track owner contributions?|Record cash introduced by owners separately from guest revenue, using the accounting treatment appropriate to the business structure. How should I record owner withdrawals?|Record transfers to owners distinctly from operating expenses and use the treatment appropriate to the entity and circumstances. What should I track about personal use of a rental?|Keep accurate dates, users, purpose where relevant, and availability changes, along with related costs and adviser guidance.
