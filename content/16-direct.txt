What is a direct booking?|A direct booking is a reservation arranged outside a marketplace's standard reservation process, typically through the operator's own channel. The operator must understand payment, guest information, terms, support, taxes, and protection arrangements. Avoid assuming that removing a platform fee removes the work the platform performs. Direct reservations need a clear operating and legal process suited to the property's location and business.|Map the reservation from inquiry to payment, stay, and resolution.
Should I launch a direct-booking website?|Evaluate whether you have lawful operations, accurate information, a workable reservation process, and a realistic way to attract appropriate guests. A website alone does not create demand. Include payment, terms, calendar, taxes, privacy, support, and coverage in the plan. Compare the total cost and work with other channels. Start only when the process can deliver what the site promises.|Build a readiness checklist before accepting reservations through the site.
What information should a direct-booking page show?|Show the actual property, capacity, sleeping arrangements, facilities, access, relevant limitations, total pricing information, terms, and a clear reservation process. Requirements vary, so obtain appropriate guidance. Keep details synchronized with other channels. An attractive page that omits material conditions can create confusion and disputes. Help guests make an informed decision instead of relying on a persuasive headline.|Audit the page against the current property specification and applicable obligations.
How do I handle payments for direct reservations?|Use a suitable payment process and understand fees, timing, refunds, disputes, security, and provider requirements. What should direct-booking terms cover?|Terms should address the reservation, pricing, payment, cancellation, guest obligations, property use, and the relevant resolution process, with appropriate legal review.
